The race organizer (organizer.glb) is baked by tools/make_drive_in_organizer.py from MakeHuman's base mesh, morph targets, default rig and system assets (young Caucasian male skin, male_casualsuit06 with its logos painted out, shoes03, hair short02, eyebrow001, eyelashes01, low-poly eyes), all released under CC0 1.0, using its MPFB add-on for Blender. MakeHuman's AGPL and MPFB's GPL cover their program code, none of which is in the game; both projects state that exported characters are the user's own data.

The crowd (people.glb: a pool of diners, the cook and the waitress) is baked by tools/make_drive_in_people.py from the same CC0 MakeHuman assets: the male1591 and female1605 proxy bodies, MakeHuman's skins, its casual, elegant and work outfits (the T-shirts repainted without their logos, the waitress's blouse and skirt repainted pink), its hair, eyebrows and eyes. The cook's toque and the waitress's cap are simple shapes made in the script.
