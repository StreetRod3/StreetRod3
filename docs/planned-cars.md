# Planned cars

Every car from Street Rod, Street Rod SE, and Street Rod 2, and every car already in Street Rod 3 that none of the three had. A checked box is a car already in the game. Cars are listed under the first game that had them, by year.

A car in more than one game is listed once. The 1963 Corvette is SR1's car 5, The King's car in SR1 and SRSE, and SR2's 1963 Corvette. SR2 has the 1969 Shelby GT500 twice, one for sale and one as The King's car. The 1950 Chevy Styleline in the game counts for SR1's 1949 Styleline: the body is the same, and only the grille differs.
