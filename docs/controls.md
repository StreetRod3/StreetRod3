# Controls

Every control in the game: keyboard, mouse, gamepad, and steering wheel. They can be used alongside one another.

## Keyboard

### Driving

| Action | Key |
| --- | --- |
| Throttle | W or Up |
| Brake | S or Down |
| Steer | A / D or Left / Right |
| Shift up / down | E / Q |
| Neutral | Q from first gear (or drive); the engine revs free on the gas |
| Reverse | Q from neutral with the car stopped |
| Hand brake | Space |
| Camera | C |
| Pause | Esc |
| Burnout | W and S with the car stopped in gear, steering to swing the back round |

These can be changed under Controls in the settings. Esc always pauses, whatever else is set.

### Mouse driving

The Mouse tab under Controls enables steering and acceleration/braking independently, with a sensitivity slider for each. Both are off by default. While driving, move the mouse left or right to steer, up to accelerate, and down to brake; move it back to return to neutral. Left-click resets steering, and right-click resets acceleration/braking. The mouse is captured during driving and released for menus. Both axes reset to neutral when driving resumes or the window loses focus. Keyboard, gamepad, and pedals remain available.

## Gamepad

Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo, and most other common gamepads work with no setup. The HUD shows the controls for the pad that is plugged in, by its own button names.

### Driving

| Action | Xbox | PlayStation | Nintendo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Steer | Left stick or D-pad | Left stick or D-pad | Left stick or D-pad |
| Gas | RT or right stick up | R2 or right stick up | ZR or right stick up |
| Brake | LT or right stick down | L2 or right stick down | ZL or right stick down |
| Shift up / down | RB / LB | R1 / L1 | R / L |
| Neutral | LB from first gear (or drive) | L1, the same way | L, the same way |
| Reverse | LB from neutral with the car stopped | L1, the same way | L, the same way |
| Hand brake | X | Square | Y |
| Camera | Y | Triangle | X |
| Pause | Start | Options | Plus |
| Burnout | Gas and brake with the car stopped in gear | The same | The same |

The triggers and sticks are gradual. On a Nintendo pad ZL and ZR are only on or off, so use the right stick to ease onto the gas. A burnout works with a trigger on one pedal and the stick on the other, such as ZL held and the right stick pushed up.

Typing a name on the license needs a keyboard, but the name can be left blank.

### Rumble

The pad rumbles for burnouts, rough ground, slides, the engine at its limit, hits, scrapes, and shifts. How hard is set under Controls in the settings, down to off.

## Changing the controls

Controls in the settings, in the main menu, the garage menu, and the race pause menu, has two tabs. Keyboard and gamepad changes the driving controls:

- Each job has two keyboard slots and two gamepad slots.
- Click a slot, then press the key, button, trigger, or stick direction for it.
- Press what is already in a slot to clear it, or Delete. Esc cancels.
- A control does one job, so using it for another moves it.
- Reset all puts every control back.

Menu buttons cannot be changed, so nobody can lock themselves out, and Esc always pauses.

## Steering wheel, pedals, and shifter

The Wheel and shifter tab under Controls in the settings binds them, since every wheel numbers its controls differently:

- Click a row, then move that control.
- Steering: turn the wheel to the right and back to center.
- Pedals and an axis hand brake: press all the way down and let go. This also learns where the pedal rests and which way it goes.
- Gears and buttons: put the shifter in that gear, or press the button.
- Each row shows a live reading to check a binding by.

How the game uses them:

- Steering sensitivity sets how far the wheel turns for full lock. At 3, a 900 degree wheel reaches full lock a third of the way round.
- An H-pattern shifter sets the gear from the lever. With no gear held the car is in neutral, including on the start line.
- On an automatic, any forward gear on the shifter is drive.
- A gear past a car's top gear is its top gear, so a 7-speed shifter works with a 4-speed car.
- Reverse at speed waits in neutral until the car has slowed.
- Paddles or a sequential shifter go on Shift up and Shift down.
- Camera and Pause can go on wheel buttons.

There is no force feedback, so the wheel only centers itself if its own settings do that.

A Moza HGP shifter through a Moza base uses buttons up around 113 to 120, which Linux may not pass on. If its gears do not register, plug the shifter into the computer with its own USB cable.

## Driving HUD

Graphics in the settings sets what the driving HUD shows and how big it is, with a preview of the race screen that changes as you go:

- Checkboxes turn each part on or off: opponent, speed, gear, damage, distance to finish, race time, control hints, and the rev counter. With every line off, the panel goes away.
- The size goes down to 60%. It shrinks the whole panel in the top right corner, text and spacing alike, and the rev counter with it, and both stay where they are.

## Reverse

Shifts step through reverse, neutral, and first (or drive on an automatic). One shift down from first or drive is neutral, and another is reverse once the car has stopped; while it still rolls forward it stays in neutral. Going into neutral takes no shift time, so a quick double tap goes from first to reverse, or back up. In neutral the gas revs the engine without moving the car, on the start line too, and gas and brake together do not start a burnout.
