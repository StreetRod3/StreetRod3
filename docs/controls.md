# Controls

The keyboard controls are in the [README](../README.md#play). Everything here can be used alongside the keyboard.

## Gamepad

Xbox, PlayStation, Nintendo, and most other common gamepads work with no setup. The HUD shows the controls for the pad that is plugged in, by its own button names.

### Driving

| Action | Xbox | PlayStation | Nintendo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Steer | Left stick or D-pad | Left stick or D-pad | Left stick or D-pad |
| Gas | RT or right stick up | R2 or right stick up | ZR or right stick up |
| Brake | LT or right stick down | L2 or right stick down | ZL or right stick down |
| Shift up / down | RB / LB | R1 / L1 | R / L |
| Reverse | LB from first gear (or drive) with the car stopped | L1, the same way | L, the same way |
| Hand brake | X | Square | Y |
| Camera | Y | Triangle | X |
| Pause | Start | Options | Plus |
| Burnout | Gas and brake with the car stopped | The same | The same |

The triggers and sticks are gradual. On a Nintendo pad ZL and ZR are only on or off, so use the right stick to ease onto the gas. A burnout works with a trigger on one pedal and the stick on the other, such as ZL held and the right stick pushed up.

### Menus and the garage

| Action | Xbox | PlayStation | Nintendo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move through a menu | D-pad | D-pad | D-pad |
| Choose | A | Cross | A |
| Back | B or Start | Circle or Options | B or Plus |
| Move the pointer | Left stick | Left stick | Left stick |
| Click with the pointer | A | Cross | A |
| Right-click, or hold and move to turn the view | X | Square | Y |
| Zoom | Right stick | Right stick | Right stick |
| Clock menu, in the garage | Back | Share | Minus |

The pointer covers the screens that take a mouse, like the garage and under the hood. Moving the real mouse takes over from it. On a Nintendo pad the button marked A chooses and B goes back, as on a Switch. The asking price for a car moves up and down by $50 with the D-pad. Typing a name on the license needs a keyboard, but the name can be left blank.

### Rumble

The pad rumbles for burnouts, rough ground, slides, the engine at its limit, hits, scrapes, and shifts. How hard is set under Controls in the settings, down to off.

## Changing the controls

Controls in the settings, in the main menu, the garage clock, and the race pause menu, changes the driving controls:

- Each job has two keyboard slots and two gamepad slots.
- Click a slot, then press the key, button, trigger, or stick direction for it.
- Press what is already in a slot to clear it, or Delete. Esc cancels.
- A control does one job, so using it for another moves it.
- Reset all puts every control back.

Menu buttons cannot be changed, so nobody can lock themselves out, and Esc always pauses.

## Steering wheel, pedals, and shifter

Wheel and shifter setup in the settings binds them, since every wheel numbers its controls differently:

- Click a row, then move that control.
- Steering: turn the wheel to the right and back to center.
- Pedals and an axis hand brake: press all the way down and let go. This also learns where the pedal rests and which way it goes.
- Gears and buttons: put the shifter in that gear, or press the button.
- Each row shows a live reading to check a binding by.

How the game uses them:

- Steering sensitivity sets how far the wheel turns for full lock. At 3, a 900 degree wheel reaches full lock a third of the way round.
- An H-pattern shifter sets the gear from the lever. With no gear held the car is in neutral, including on the start line.
- On an automatic, any forward gear on the shifter is drive.
- A gear past a car's top gear is its top gear, so a 7-speed shifter works with a 4-speed car.
- Reverse at speed waits in neutral until the car has slowed.
- Paddles or a sequential shifter go on Shift up and Shift down.
- Camera and Pause can go on wheel buttons.

There is no force feedback, so the wheel only centers itself if its own settings do that.

A Moza HGP shifter through a Moza base uses buttons up around 113 to 120, which Linux may not pass on. If its gears do not register, plug the shifter into the computer with its own USB cable.

## Reverse

One shift down from first, or from drive on an automatic, is reverse once the car has stopped. While it is still rolling forward, that shift is neutral, and another once it has slowed is reverse. One shift up from reverse is first.
