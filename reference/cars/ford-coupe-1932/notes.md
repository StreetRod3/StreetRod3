# 1932 Ford coupe (customized)

The game's model is `assets/cars/sr1-ford-customized-coupe-1932.glb`, converted from Street Rod 1's customized coupe: a highboy hot rod with no fenders or bumpers and a chopped top. Its wheels were about a third too far apart; `tools/rescale_car_glb.py` scaled it (width ×0.889, height unchanged, length ×0.759). The length is set by the real wheelbase and the width by the real track, since the stock car's overall length and width count bumpers and fenders this car doesn't have. The height was left alone: 58 in to the top suits a chopped and lowered coupe, against roughly 66–69 in stock.
