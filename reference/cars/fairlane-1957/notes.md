# 1957 Ford Fairlane 500

The game's model is `assets/cars/sr1-fairlane-1957.glb`, converted from Street Rod 1's Fairlane. It came out about a fifth too long and more than a third too wide; `tools/rescale_car_glb.py` scaled it to the real car (width ×0.722, height ×0.898, length ×0.817, the length set by the wheelbase so the arches stay over the wheels).
