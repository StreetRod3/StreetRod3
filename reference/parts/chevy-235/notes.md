# Chevy 235 / 216 inline six

| Path | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `model/` | The SketchUp model of a 235 the layout was measured from (`tools/make_235.py`): `model.dae` as exported, `model.gltf` (272 MB) and `model.glb` made from it, `textures/` its images; copied from `~/Downloads/Chevrolet235` |
| `renders/` | Renders of that model from every side |
| `renders-grid/` | Orthographic renders with a grid; `meta.json` gives each view's camera (model units) |
| `manual/` | Scanned pages of the 1960 235-261 engine manual: section 8 (`p8-01`…`p8-31`), page 7-16, Fuel & Exhaust (`fe1`…`fe6`) and Specifications (`spec-16-…`); checked against for the stock valve cover, the combined intake/exhaust manifold and the piston heights |
| `valve-cover-finned/` | Product photo of the finned aluminum valve cover (the higher-performance head) |
| `intake-2x1/` | Product photo of the two one-barrel intake |
| `headers/` | Product photo of the split cast-iron headers |
| `exhaust-manifold/` | Photos of the stock exhaust manifold (firing order 1-5-3-6-2-4 cast in) |

Photos pasted 2026-11-11 and 2026-11-12. The SketchUp model's original source and license: not recorded.
